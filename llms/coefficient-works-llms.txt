## Overall
Coefficient is a no‑code platform that connects Google Sheets and Excel to 60+ business systems like Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Netsuite, Snowflake, MySQL, Google Ads and more. Coefficient AI Assistant also enables AI on your Google Sheets to create pivot tables, formulas, charts, cleaning data and more using natural language. Used by 50000+ companies for spreadsheet automation and data management.
